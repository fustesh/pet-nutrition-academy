Understanding Cat Nutrition

Cats are obligate carnivores, meaning they require a diet high in animal-based proteins and specific nutrients like taurine and arachidonic acid.

Wet food is often preferable due to cats' low thirst drive. Ensure you're feeding high-quality protein sources without unnecessary fillers.
